## Important limitation

This is a **2.5D scene-compositing enhancement**, not a real-time 3D model engine. Original Harbour buildings exist in the static background from Level 1, with perspective-matched 3D-looking additions at Levels 2 and 3. Other districts still use their existing illustrations and old SVG upgrades. A fully dynamic, destructible/buildable 3D town with complete Level 1→3 architecture across six districts requires a dedicated 3D art pipeline. Do not describe this milestone as completed true 3D.

The full v1.4.1 editable source and art generator have been packaged as a source ZIP. No Google Drive upload of this release was independently verified.
